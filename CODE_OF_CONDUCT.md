# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0), summarised here.

**Our pledge.** We want participation in Folia to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

**Expected behaviour:** be kind and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, take responsibility for mistakes,
and focus on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable behaviour:** harassment, insults, trolling, sexualised language or imagery, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns through the
repository's private security/contact channel described in `SECURITY.md`, or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub.
